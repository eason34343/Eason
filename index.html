UX prototype / Digital accessibility

Helping older adults complete one screen at a time.

OneStep is a mobile-sized, clickable prototype for an AI visual guide that breaks smartphone tasks into a calm sequence: one screen, one decision, one step at a time. It explores how instructions can reduce cognitive load without pretending the prototype controls a real phone.

03 / About me

I build around
the person using it.

I’m Eason, an independent student web designer. I enjoy the part of a project that happens before the finished page: listening, sorting out what matters, and deciding what can be removed.

My goal is a website that feels straightforward to the people a business wants to reach. Every page should answer a question, build understanding, or help someone take the next step.
